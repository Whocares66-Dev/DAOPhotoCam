# Photo Cam

A free camera for screenshots in Dragon Age: Origins. Turn it on, and the world stops: fly anywhere, frame the shot, and take it.

![A fight frozen mid-leap, seen from beside the party]({{ "/assets/img/frozen-fight.jpg" | relative_url }}){: .screenshot loading="lazy"}

## Features

- **Fly freely**: look with the mouse, move with <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd>, rise and sink with <kbd>Q</kbd> <kbd>E</kbd>. No snap to the tactical view, however far you go.
- **The world paused**: in a fight too. Nothing moves while you look for the shot.
- **Field of view** from 15° to 120°, and a speed from 0.5 to 40 m/s.
- **[A panel]({{ '/panel/' | relative_url }})** in the game's own style, for the lock, the field of view, the speed and the shot. It is never in a screenshot.
- **[A camera button in the top bar]({{ '/panel/' | relative_url }}#the-top-bar)** to turn it on.
- **Back where you were**: off, the camera returns to the party, and the game carries on.
